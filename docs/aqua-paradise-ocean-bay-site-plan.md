# Ocean Bay — Coastal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** conceptual layout accepted by user, 2026-10-27. **Not scaled, surveyed or engineered.**
**Geography:** large protected natural crescent-shaped marine bay on the island's remote **northeastern coast**, distinct from Paradise Grand Hotel's secluded northwestern shoreline. **52 ha of land** assigned to Ocean Bay; designated marine waters are **not included** in 52 ha or total island land area of 600 ha.
**Sources:** `docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md`; functional authority `docs/website-requirements.md` §7.7.7.

## Concept
A broad sandy public beach and landscape-first ocean resort district. The large natural marine bay remains a real beach, **not an artificial lagoon replacing it**. Protected headlands allow ocean vistas and controlled supervised sea recreation. Public Paradise Loop access and onshore promenades, separate rescue/service access, independent hotel-private beach.

## Approved conceptual arrangement
- **Paradise Beach:** broad central sandy crescent, loungers and shaded rest along shore; largest portion of land.
- **Ocean Rush Complex:** coastal slope at one side of bay with safe queue, operations and distinct controlled run-out/splashdown areas; **Ocean Rush**, **Blue Horizon**, **Tidal Drop** are its three separate slides.
- **Sunset Lounge:** elevated coastal terrace on the other side for ocean views and evening relaxation.
- **Aqua Challenge:** designated floating obstacle-course water parcel away from ordinary swimming, with small safe onshore support.
- **Coral Discovery:** designated calm instructor-supervised snorkeling parcel; no animal contact.
- **Ocean Explorer:** designated guided SUP/kayak route closer to sheltered bay opening, separated from swimmers and other activity sectors.
- Onshore lifeguard posts, planted walkways, accessible approaches, equipment and emergency access. Exact sea boundaries and routes are **not** settled.

## Canonical functions and hours

| Activity | Hours | Visitor restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| Paradise Beach shoreline | 09:00–22:00 | Sea **swimming only 09:00–19:00**, within supervised designated area; under-12 swimmers accompanied |
| Aqua Challenge | 10:00–18:00 | 8+ years, at least 120 cm; under-12 adult accompanied; buoyancy vest required |
| Coral Discovery | 10:00–17:00 | 10+ years, under-16 accompanied, basic swimming skills; instructor supervision |
| Ocean Explorer | 10:00–18:00 | 12+ years, under-16 accompanied, basic swimming skills, life jacket; instructor-supervised paddling route |
| Sunset Lounge | 16:00–22:00 | Calm evening oceanside dining / relaxation; allowed with Evening Pass |
| Ocean Rush Complex | 10:00–18:00 | Each slide has distinct limits: Ocean Rush 10+ and ≥120 cm; Blue Horizon 12+ and ≥140 cm; Tidal Drop 16+ and ≥150 cm |

**Ocean Rush** is a panoramic winding slide; **Blue Horizon** two parallel race slides; **Tidal Drop** an extreme tower drop. All finish in **engineered, supervised splashdown compartments**, not in open unrestricted sea. The slide-water treatment / physical separation requires engineering; the resort's coast remains natural.

After 19:00, no sea swimming; coastal walks and Sunset Lounge can continue within their own hours. Weather and sea conditions affect marine operations: demo website does not assert live wave/current/safety conditions. Eligibility and accompaniment in the itinerary follow canonical requirements.
