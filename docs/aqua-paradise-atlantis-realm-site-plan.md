# Atlantis Realm — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** conceptual layout accepted 2026-10-27; land allocation approved as a working layout, but not scaled or engineered.
**Island masterplan:** [aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md](./aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md). **Functional authority:** [website-requirements.md](./website-requirements.md), §7.7.5.

## Concept
The **60-ha** Atlantis Realm district is one immersive lost underwater city, not four unrelated slide stations. Architectural identity: antique temple ruins, decorative canals, bridges, columns, shaded galleries and scenic water features. The shared public **central ruined-city square** ties attractions together and supports orientation.

These are planning allocations rather than actual structural footprints. Avoid double-counting facilities in common-island infrastructure land.

### Entry and central pedestrian flow
Entrance from the adjacent main-island pedestrian network → welcoming Moonlit Ruins area → central ruined-city plaza → diverging routes east/west/north. Provide transverse connections, safe access, emergency service access separated from pedestrian paths, queues and accessible alternative routes. Final distances, slopes and paths require scaled site mapping.

## Confirmed ride facts from Website Requirements
| Attraction | Minimum age | Minimum height | Hours |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Poseidon's Descent | 14 | 140 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Tidal Kingdom | 6 | 110 cm | 10:00–21:00 |
| Crystal Passage | 4 | 100 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Moonlit Ruins | 8 | 120 cm | 10:00–21:00 |

Under-10s require adult accompaniment at Tidal Kingdom, Crystal Passage, and Moonlit Ruins; Poseidon's Descent has no on-ride adult accompaniment requirement, but overall minor attendance restrictions continue to apply.

- **Poseidon's Descent:** swift launch from Poseidon temple, enclosed tunnel and curved descents.
- **Tidal Kingdom:** large wave basin. **Waves stop at 19:00**, pool continues calmer with decorative lighting to 21:00.
- **Crystal Passage:** two-person capsules float along their own channel through a transparent tunnel **surrounded by a separate real tropical-fish aquarium**. Aquarium and ride water do not mix; animal welfare systems and separate staff areas required. No general-public external aquarium viewing gallery: visitors only view live fish during the ride.
- **Moonlit Ruins:** calm themed pool in columns/waterfalls; evening illumination 19:00–21:00.

MVP does not simulate live real-world ride conditions. No fabricated queue lengths or guaranteed accessibility.
