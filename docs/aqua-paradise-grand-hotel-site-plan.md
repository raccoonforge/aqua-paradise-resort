# Paradise Grand Hotel & Villas — Site Plan v1.0

Status: user-approved conceptual layout, 2026-10-27. Not a scaled or engineered map.
Related: docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md, docs/project-bible.md, docs/aqua-paradise-hotel-blueprint.md.

## Location and concept
The hotel occupies a **remote 52-ha site on the northwestern coast** of the fictional Aqua Paradise island, around a sheltered hotel bay. Its grounds and beach are distinct from public Ocean Bay on the northeastern coast. The current location supersedes an outdated note in Project Bible describing the hotel in the first half of the island.

Architecture: terraced tropical five-star resort on a gentle coastal slope, landscaped gardens, ocean outlooks, quiet villas, private guest spaces, and transport links avoiding public shortcuts across villa grounds.

## Site organization
- Upper main terrace: Paradise Grand Hotel, primary reception and arrival court.
- Family cluster near main hotel: Paradise Family Suites.
- More secluded seaside garden plots: Ocean View Villas.
- Separated private plots: Royal Paradise Villas with private pools.
- Shore/terrace: Grand Infinity Pool and calm coastal leisure.
- Shared amenity buildings or main hotel: Paradise Dining, Grand Kids Club, Grand Wellness & Fitness.
- Paradise Loop access at public-facing hotel forecourt plus separate guest/luggage transfer and operational/emergency approaches.
