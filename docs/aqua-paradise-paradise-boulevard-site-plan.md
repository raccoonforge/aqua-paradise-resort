# Paradise Boulevard — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** approved conceptual layout 2026-10-27; not scaled or engineered.
**Area:** 35 hectares on the narrow southern arrival end of Aqua Paradise. Canonical functions: `docs/website-requirements.md` §7.7.10; island context: `docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md`.

## Vision and circulation
The resort's grand welcome district, creating a luxurious tropical arrival experience without sacrificing crowd circulation. Visitors from Ocean Wings cable car and marine ferry/speedboat terminals pass through **Paradise Gateway**, into the wide **Palm Avenue**, past the **Paradise Fountain Show**, then continue north to Paradise Market and Eden Gardens. The Island Welcome Center and Paradise Loop Central Station are accessible from the main arrival flow yet must not obstruct pedestrians or fountain spectators. Service and transport roads are physically separated from the main promenade as feasible.

## Conceptual placement
- **South:** Paradise Gateway, signature arch, arrival plaza, photo locations and clear entry/ticket control.
- **Western side:** Island Welcome Center with visitor information, resort maps and demonstration kiosks.
- **Eastern side:** Paradise Loop Central Station, shaded boarding/waiting area with two-direction shuttle departures.
- **Central axis:** Palm Avenue, palm-lined step-free main public promenade with shady seating, landscape and water features.
- **Central plaza off the primary pedestrian flow:** Paradise Fountain Show with appropriately separated standing/viewing spaces.
- **North:** direct connection to Paradise Market; preserve continuous through-traffic and emergency/access routes.

## Confirmed public facts and schedule
- **Paradise Gateway:** 09:00–22:00; arrival / photography (suggested 10–15 minutes).
- **Island Welcome Center:** 09:00–22:00; information (10–20 minutes suggested), not a real staff contact service in the MVP.
- **Palm Avenue:** 09:00–22:00; leisurely walk (15–30 minutes suggested); passing through it does **not** automatically count as an attraction visit in Planner.
- **Paradise Fountain Show:** decorative operation 10:00–19:00; two actual scheduled shows **19:30–19:45** and **21:00–21:15**. Show area 10:00–21:30. Each 15-minute show may need 10 minutes advance arrival. Included in ticket; compatible with Evening Pass.
- **Paradise Loop Central Station:** 08:30–22:30; free transportation, both ring directions, estimated waiting 5–15 minutes (not a guaranteed headway). A logistics node rather than an attraction.

All site attractions are included in demo admission, follow overall guest accompaniment rules, and should have accessible circulation. The district is 09:00–22:00 generally; transit may operate outside the general admission period to accommodate arrivals/departures.
