# Eden Gardens — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** accepted conceptual plan on 2026-10-27; precise polygons, distances, transit nodes and detailed environmental engineering are not yet defined.
**Area:** 60 ha, at the island's central green heart. Sources: `docs/website-requirements.md` §7.7.9 and `docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md`.

## Key concept
A full-sized tropical botanical park that is **both** a destination and a central pedestrian interchange. The interior of the island must not be empty. Two circulation types coexist: a practical, direct and accessible transit spine/cross-links between adjacent districts; and slower scenic paths among botanical displays, ponds, waterfalls and shaded gardens. Neither should force detours through paid or time-restricted exhibits. No Paradise Loop stop in the protected core; visitors reach the park from peripheral district stops and pedestrian links.

## Approved conceptual spatial arrangement
- **Emerald Walk**: primary public botanical promenade / central axial garden, with safe stroller and limited-mobility paths.
- **Hidden Falls**: scenic waterfall at the northern side, lookout and protected water edge; **bathing prohibited**.
- **Butterfly Haven**: enclosed living-butterfly conservatory on the western side, separate controlled-care and technical spaces.
- **Orchid Pavilion**: orchid/botanical exhibit in the eastern area.
- **Starlight Trail**: southern side and connected evening garden path, safe decorative lighting after dusk.
- **Central green meeting square**: wayfinding, shaded rest, junction for cross-island paths; not a separate ticketed attraction.
- Several central and lateral footpath connections; emergency/service/accessible routing as needed without creating a vehicular thoroughfare through the botanical core.

## Existing functional rules
| Site | Hours | Special rules |
|---|---|---|
| Emerald Walk | 09:00–21:00 | No minimum age; stroller and accessible route in the site requirements |
| Hidden Falls | 09:00–19:00 | Viewing only; swimming forbidden, protective water edge |
| Butterfly Haven | 10:00–18:00 | Living butterflies; children under 12 accompanied by an adult; no touching, feeding or flash photography |
| Orchid Pavilion | 09:00–19:00 | Do not damage/remove plants; accessible route |
| Starlight Trail | 17:00–21:00 | Evening lighting; children under 12 accompanied by an adult; Evening Pass permitted within hours |

All five sites included in admission; no separate bookings in MVP. More general accompaniment and park rules remain in force. No invented real-time closure/weather/path status.
