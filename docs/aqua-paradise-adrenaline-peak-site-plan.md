# Adrenaline Peak — Internal Area Plan v1.0

**Status:** user accepted as conceptual working layout ("сойдет"), 2026-10-27. **Not** a scaled architectural drawing, attraction construction specification, or final precise polygon geometry.

**Zone:** Adrenaline Peak, the remote elevated northern inland region of Aqua Paradise Island Resort; total approved land allocation **65 hectares**. Site-wide layout: [Island Masterplan](./aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md).

## Spatial concept

The entry and Paradise Loop access are downhill to the south, leading to a visitor orientation/service square, then branching paths and ramps/accessible alternatives climb to four large aquatic attraction complexes. **Eagle's Lookout** is located at the high panoramic part of the district. The dramatic hill forms a focal point visible from elsewhere on the island.

Illustrative placement, not to scale:
- **North/high point:** Eagle's Lookout;
- **Upper western slope:** Skyfall, with nearly vertical start-tower drop;
- **Upper eastern slope:** Velocity Racers, six parallel competition lanes;
- **Mid-west:** Cyclone Vortex, high-intensity group raft experience;
- **Mid-east:** Thunder Rush, two-person raft descents and uphill sections;
- **Southern entrance:** visitor plaza with rest points, information, safe circulation and nearby transit connection.

Actual lengths, elevations, footprints, grade and splashdown conditions are to be technically validated; do not infer them from the schematic circles.

## Authoritative ride data (Website Requirements §7.7.6)

| Facility | Minimum age | Minimum height | Regular operation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Skyfall | 16 | 150 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Velocity Racers | 12 | 140 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Cyclone Vortex | 14 | 140 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Thunder Rush | 14 | 145 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Eagle's Lookout | no minimum | no minimum | 09:00–20:00 |

- Skyfall: transparent-door single launch capsule, countdown, near-vertical high-speed plunge; extreme intensity.
- Velocity Racers: six parallel water racing lanes; high intensity.
- Cyclone Vortex: group raft ride (maximum 4 occupants), high intensity.
- Thunder Rush: alternating rapid drops and ascents, two-person rafts; extreme intensity. Never promise single-rider permission or guaranteed pairing.
- Eagle's Lookout: calm panoramic observation platform, adult accompaniment for children younger than 12.
- Water rides close at 19:00. The observation point stays accessible until 20:00 subject to its own rules; Premium or Priority Access does not bypass height/age/safety requirements.
- Full ride descriptions, further rules and durations live in `docs/website-requirements.md`, which is the canonical functional source.
