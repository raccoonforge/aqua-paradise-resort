# Pirate's Cove — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** user-approved conceptual working layout, 2026-10-27; this is not a scaled engineering plan.
**Zone footprint:** 52 ha on the northeastern inner side of the island, adjacent to Little Paradise and accessible from main pedestrian paths / Paradise Loop district stop.
**Authoritative attractions and rules:** `docs/website-requirements.md`, section 7.7.4.
**Island-wide context:** `docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md`.

## Core vision
Pirate's Cove feels like a single immersive pirate harbor, not four disconnected water rides. At its center is the multi-deck pirate ship **Stormbreaker**, surrounded by themed harbor water, wooden fortifications, rock formations, waterfalls, pirate passages and walkable public squares. Visitors approach from a southern pirate-port entrance, follow clear paths toward the ship and branch to activity districts. Harbor water features are themed installations, **not** an authorization for unrestricted sea swimming.

## Conceptual orientation
- **Southern entrance:** thematic port arrival plaza, accessible visitor routing, wayfinding and service points.
- **Central harbor:** **Stormbreaker** as dominant visual landmark, family water-play and crowd circulation.
- **Western elevated area:** **Blackbeard's Run** speed slide with enclosed sections and turns.
- **Eastern rocky/passage area:** **Smuggler's Passage** calm inflatable-ring water route through smugglers' caves and waterfalls.
- **Near entrance / southeastern area:** **Cannonball Clash** interactive water-battle arena with water cannons and team fortifications.
- Direct connections to nearby Little Paradise and wider island network must not create uncontrolled conflicts between water attractions, queues and general pedestrian flows. Service, rescue and equipment access separate from pedestrian passages.

The allocations include attraction-specific queue areas, controlled-water play and technical/safety buffers. Exact boundaries, structure footprints, water volumes, slopes and widths are TBD rather than implied by a schematic.

## Confirmed attraction data (Website Requirements)
| Ride | Minimum age | Minimum height | Operating hours |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Stormbreaker | 6 years | 110 cm | 10:00–20:00, main slides only until 19:00 |
| Blackbeard's Run | 12 years | 140 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Smuggler's Passage | 5 years | 100 cm | 10:00–19:00 |
| Cannonball Clash | 7 years | 120 cm | 10:00–20:00 |

Children under 10 need adult accompaniment on Stormbreaker, Smuggler's Passage and Cannonball Clash; not required on Blackbeard's Run by its per-ride rule. The broader under-18 group accompaniment rule still applies. Age, height, operating-hours and safe accompaniment are binding, even with Premium Pass or Priority Access. One adult cannot accompany children at two attractions concurrently. No invented live queue conditions.

**Evening handling:** 19:00–20:00 only dedicated evening-safe play sections of Stormbreaker and illuminated Cannonball Clash remain available; main Stormbreaker slides close at 19:00 along with Blackbeard's Run and Smuggler's Passage.
