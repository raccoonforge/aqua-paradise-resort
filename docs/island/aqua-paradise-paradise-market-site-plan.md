# Paradise Market — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** conceptual internal layout accepted by user on 2026-10-27 ("пойдет"). Not scaled or engineered.
**Zone area:** 25 hectares, between Paradise Boulevard (south) and Eden Gardens (north), inside the ticketed resort. Refer to `docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md` and `docs/website-requirements.md`, §7.7.11.

## District concept and pedestrian layout
A realistic, landscaped open-air resort commercial and gastronomy district: central walkable street, shaded retail terraces, fountains, places to sit, and evening illumination. **Primary north-south public promenade remains a clear thoroughfare** between Paradise Boulevard and Eden Gardens, without restaurant queuing or merchandise obstructing the route. Secondary east-west access supports other zones and emergency/service deliveries. No independent free public entry to this ticketed district.

**Sunset Market Square** forms the public heart, while dining and retail are distributed along both sides. The tentative placements, not scaled:
- **Northwest:** Azure Terrace Restaurant, on an elevated site whose sea view must be checked against actual island topography (do not promise the view can be achieved before checking).
- **Northeast:** Island Treasures souvenir store.
- **Southwest:** Tropical Bites Food Hall, convenient for lunch/family groups.
- **Southeast:** Splash Essentials beach gear store.
- **Southern or along the main spine:** Coconut & Co. cafe for short breaks.
- **Central:** Sunset Market Square, landscaping and pedestrian plaza.
These directional positions are working design choices, subordinate to coast visibility, transport and accessible circulation in scaled design.
