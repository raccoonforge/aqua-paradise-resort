# Little Paradise — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** user approved conceptual layout on 2026-10-27. This document is not an engineered, scaled map.
**Zone allocation:** 42 hectares, eastern inner/southeastern family area near Pirate's Cove.
**Authority:** `docs/website-requirements.md`, section 7.7.3, and `docs/aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md`.

## Vision and spatial arrangement
Little Paradise is a substantial family-oriented waterpark district with **three physically distinguishable age zones**, not one undifferentiated pool. Spatial sequence from district entrance to the Pirate's Cove side:

1. **Tiny Tides**, near the family entrance and rest/service amenities: quiet shallow-water toddler play (ages 0–3), small fountains and gentle elements; adult accompaniment mandatory.
2. **Rainbow Reef**, central visual family hub: colorful small water slides, mild obstacles and shallow pools (ages 4–7), minimum 90 cm; accompaniment required for ages 4–5.
3. **Splash Explorers**, farther from main entry, closest to Pirate's Cove: more dynamic slide/adventure play (ages 8–12), minimum 110 cm; accompaniment required for ages 8–9.

Visitors and adults may accompany children where the rules allow but are not independent users of age-restricted play equipment. Family resting places, shade and sanitary services must be accessible from all three areas. Circulation should respect childcare safety and prevent inadvertent access to inappropriate activity sections. Adjacent Pirate's Cove is a separate district with its own restrictions.

## Existing confirmed rules
- **Tiny Tides**: age 0–3, no minimum height, adult accompany always, 10:00–19:00, very low intensity; recommended 20–30 minutes.
- **Rainbow Reef**: age 4–7, minimum 90 cm, adult accompany for children younger than 6, 10:00–19:00, low intensity; recommended 30–45 minutes.
- **Splash Explorers**: age 8–12, minimum 110 cm, adult accompany for children younger than 10, 10:00–19:00, moderate intensity; recommended 45–60 minutes.
- Date-specific age, height, and real availability of accompanying adults must be checked by `/planner`. A single adult cannot simultaneously supervise children at different stations.
- General park rules on under-18 visitor accompaniment remain applicable and are independent of the on-playground rules.

## Service and mobility
Provide convenient access to toilets, family changing, stroller parking, shaded resting points and first-aid facilities without unverified promises about exact counts or paths. Design safe emergency/service separation and step-free alternatives subject to future site engineering.
