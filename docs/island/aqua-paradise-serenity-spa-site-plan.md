# Serenity Spa — Internal Site Plan v1.0

**Status:** conceptual layout accepted by user on 2026-10-27; not to scale, not an engineered facility plan.
**Zone land budget:** 35 hectares in the island's western / southwestern inner district near Eden Gardens, as in `aqua-paradise-island-masterplan.md`.
**Canonical object details:** `website-requirements.md`, §7.7.8.

## Vision
A low-density tropical wellness sanctuary, with lush vegetation, winding walkways, warm pools, waterfalls, secluded massage pavilions and quiet meditation gardens. Built structures should not dominate the landscape. Distinct quiet/temperature areas and clear, safe pedestrian access.

## Agreed conceptual arrangement
- **Entrance (southern approach):** visitor orientation and transition from public island circulation.
- **Lotus Thermal Pools:** central heart, with warm pools of varying temperatures and shaded loungers.
- **Whispering Falls:** tranquil eastern/southeastern waterfall pool, evening lighting, scenic walkway connected to Lotus.
- **Bamboo Harmony:** more secluded western massage pavilions with a plant buffer from main circulation.
- **Mist Sanctuary:** separately buffered northeastern saunas and steam rooms.
- **Zen Garden:** quiet, farther/northern garden close to Eden Gardens natural green connections, with walking/yoga areas.
- Separate operational access, sanitation, emergency support and accessible pedestrian routes as required by eventual scaled engineering.
